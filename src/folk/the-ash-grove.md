"The Ash Grove" (Welsh: *Llwyn Onn*) is a traditional Welsh folk song whose melody
has been a staple of harp and piano repertoire for well over a century. The tune
was published in its best-known form by the Welsh harpist John Thomas in the 19th
century, though the melody itself is considerably older.

The song is a waltz-time air. The most familiar English lyrics, beginning "Down
yonder green valley where streamlets meander," were written for the tune rather
than being the original Welsh words — *Llwyn Onn* tells of a grove of ash trees
and a lost love, a common theme in Welsh *cerdd dant* tradition.

This simple piano arrangement keeps the melody in the right hand over an
alternating-octave bass, the way the tune is often first taught to piano
students. It sits comfortably in D major and rewards a gentle, singing touch.
